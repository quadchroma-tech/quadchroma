## 1. Why this Agreement exists, and why it works per contribution

QuadChroma is published under the PolyForm Strict License 1.0.0 with the Licensor's
Additional Terms (`LICENSE.txt`). That license lets people use the software, but not
change it, distribute it or sublicense it. A contribution offered "under the project
license" would therefore give the Licensor nothing he could merge, build and ship. The
Licensor also wants to offer QuadChroma under other terms - free of charge to private
users, under paid licenses to businesses - and needs the same freedom for contributed
code. Hence this Agreement.

**Why per contribution.** This Agreement is written for German copyright law (the
German Copyright Act, UrhG), which governs the Licensor and many contributors. Under
that law, a contract in which an author promises rights in future, unspecified works
requires written form with a handwritten signature and can be terminated after five
years (§ 40 UrhG), and a contract covering types of use that are not yet known requires
written form as well (§ 31a UrhG). A commit trailer or a pull request comment is not
written form. This Agreement therefore does not ask for rights in future work: it applies
separately to each Contribution, at the moment that Contribution is Submitted, and only
for types of use known at that moment. A license in an existing work for known types of
use needs no particular form, so your `Signed-off-by` trailer and your sentence in the
pull request are enough. Copyright itself cannot be transferred under German law
(§ 29(1) UrhG); accordingly, you keep it and grant a license, that is, a right of use
(§ 31 UrhG).
